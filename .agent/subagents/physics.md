# Subagent: Ball Physics

## Mission

Design the rolling/contact/integration path without changing the mechanical model.

## Deliverable

- state variables;
- force model;
- contact model;
- friction model;
- timestep method;
- energy diagnostics;
- stability detector;
- invariants/tests.

## Must distinguish

- world gravity;
- platform normal;
- tangential gravity;
- platform motion;
- ball motion.
